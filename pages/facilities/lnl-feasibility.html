      Details

      Two opposite arms of the MX17 apparatus, which ran at CERN n_TOF in 2026 (a Micromegas TPC, a SiPM scintillator wall and plastic scintillators per arm), sit around a thin Li target and record ⁷Li(p,e⁺e⁻)⁸Be pairs.

      • Why tracking. The opening angle comes from hit positions and cosmic rays are rejected by their straight tracks: the two points at issue in the ATOMKI result (MEG II, 2026).
      • What the run delivers. Rates and backgrounds on beam, a calibration on the 441 keV resonance (no anomaly there; needs an H₂⁺ beam from the CN, conditional), and a first look at 18.15 MeV near the ATOMKI level. The results set the design of the four-arm experiment (slot 2).
      • The document. Official LNL template: a one-page abstract and a body of at most 4 pages. Red brackets mark open items; the page plan is in the Proposal tab.
      • This page. Timeline, set-up, beam plan and reach, tasks, budget, risks and questions are in the tabs above. Machine sheet: CN and AN2000 beams ↗.

      The rules

        What the feasibility run proves

        1 · The apparatus on a proton beam. Trigger and singles rates vs current, DREAM live time, Micromegas occupancy, the Li target's behaviour. Everything the Geant4 reach assumes, measured.
        2 · A calibration at 441 keV. The 17.64 MeV M1 pairs, where ATOMKI saw no anomaly: angular resolution, acceptance and energy scale against Geant4. ~140 selected pairs per day of beam above 90° with two arms and the Cu hemisphere: in the run's 8 h of H₂⁺: 46 pairs, the rate to ~18 %. The CN starts at 0.8 MV, so this needs H₂⁺ at 0.883 MV (two 0.441 MeV protons): asked for, not relied on.
        3 · The backgrounds. Cosmic nights with the arms vertical before the beam: the 140° cosmic peak MEG II pointed at, measured with and without each veto, and the arm-to-arm time of flight. Accidentals and external pairs with beam.
        4 · A first look at 18.15 MeV. Five days at 1.10 MeV, two opposite arms, the built Cu hemisphere: an expected 90 % CL limit of 2.4 × R(ATOMKI) with the liquid cells working, 3.1 × as at n_TOF, 4.3 × without them; MEG II's limit is 2.07 ×. A lighter Al end piece: 0.9–1.2 × (Geant4, R10). Geant4; see Beam & reach.

        3. Cosmic nights (before the beam)

        Opening angle of two-track cosmic pairs

        The angle the analysis computes (both Micromegas charge centroids seen from the beam spot), per 10-hour night, log scale. Shaded: the X17 window 125–160°. Vertical arms: the MEG II “140° structure” at full strength.

        Arm-to-arm time of flight

        Δt between the two SiPM walls (vertical: lower − upper; horizontal: −x − +x), with the measured σt = ns per arm. Every vertical muon is downward-going: one peak at +2.3 ns. Beam pairs sit at 0.

        Pointing of the cosmic segments

        Distance of closest approach of one arm's segment to the beam spot, vertical pair: true segment and with 15° (electron-like) and 3° (muon, as on the bench) direction resolution. The cut is mm at 15°, mm at 3°.

        Muon zenith angle

        True zenith angle of the muons making two-track pairs, per night. The two orientations see different muons: near-vertical (vertical pair) and near-horizontal (side arms). The n_TOF A–C pairs (side-by-side arms) had a median of 74°, as the simulation.

        4. Beam singles (day 1: current scan)

        Trigger legs per arm against beam current

        Beam γ (8Be lines) plus cosmics, one side arm. The step from 1.10 MeV to 441 keV is the resonance. The slope is γ yield × leg probability: it measures the film's Li content and the arm's threshold.
