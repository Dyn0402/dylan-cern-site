What we ask for

An X17 feasibility study (Pierfrancesco, 18 Nov: not "detector tests"): ~8 beam days, protons at 0.441 and 1.10 MeV, ≤ 1 µA, on AN2000, with two opposite arms of the n_TOF apparatus around a thin metallic Li target. A positive answer starts the 2-branch full detector, and the results back the INFN commission request.

    Decisions to make now

      What the feasibility run proves

      1 · The apparatus on a proton beam. Trigger and singles rates vs current, DREAM live time, Micromegas occupancy, the Li target's behaviour. Everything the Geant4 reach assumes, measured.
      2 · A calibration at 441 keV. The 17.64 MeV M1 pairs, where ATOMKI saw no anomaly: angular resolution, acceptance and energy scale against Geant4. 12–24k selected pairs in two days with two arms.
      3 · The backgrounds. Beam-off cosmics with and without the track veto (MEG II's 2026 point about ATOMKI-type spectrometers), accidentals, external pairs.
      4 · A first look at 18.15 MeV. Five days at 1.10 MeV: an expected 90 % CL limit of 1.5–1.8 × R(ATOMKI) with walls and plastics alone, ~0.8 × with two liquid cells. Scaling estimate; see Beam & reach.

      What 8 days can reach

      A scaling estimate, not a simulation: the four-arm Geant4 reach of FEASIBILITY_SIM.md §19.3 (reference fit, background shape floating) ×2 in days for two opposite arms (band ×1.7–2.5). Expected limit = 1.28 / Z(ATOMKI) × R(ATOMKI). The 0.3 ns arm-to-arm time of flight is unmeasured; "pointing only" uses §19.4's ratio. A two-arm re-reduction of the L10–L13 tables (task R1) replaces this before the proposal is final. A horizontal pair of arms sees far fewer cosmics than this assumes.
