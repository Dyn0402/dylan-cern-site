Where else can MX17 go?

X17 facility search · the n_TOF apparatus on other beams · started September 2026

The MX17 apparatus took data at CERN n_TOF in 2026. Two routes could host it elsewhere: neutrons on ³He, the n_TOF reaction, or protons on lithium or tritium, the ATOMKI reaction. Each route has a dashboard; the facilities studied so far are listed below them. Work in progress, unlisted.

No cold or thermal beam beats ILL PF1B, and it is out of reach of the detector's rate limits. Continuous, soft fast sources (Birmingham, FRANZ, FRM II) reach the ATOMKI strength in 2–4 days of beam.

Hardware studies every destination needs

  • Trigger-scintillator acceptance As built the stack accepts 2.3 % of X17 pairs; four ~75 cm plastics give ~14 %.
  • ⁴He bag and the ³He-tight skin Air ¹⁴N, ³He permeation, and the PET/Al foil that stops it.
  • Budget: what it takes to run again Every component, borrow or buy, priority and cost; open questions marked.
  • Trigger and DAQ away from n_TOF Interactive: the modules (N979 split, N1081B logic, V1742/V1730S, DT5495 FPGA, FERS), signal flow, DREAM busy veto, live time, remote power, cost.
  • Funding: who can apply for what EU, Italian and US schemes, eligibility for each of us, deadlines and open questions.

The source of all of this is the x17_facility_search repository (GitHub Dyn0402/x17_facility_search); the detector geometry lives in MX17_Full_Geant and MX17_Geant. The cards and mini-dashboards are drawn from data/facilities-survey.json, written by survey/dashboard.py .
