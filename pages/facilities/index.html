Where else can MX17 go?

X17 facility studies · the n_TOF apparatus on other beams · started September 2026

The MX17 apparatus — a ³He target, four arms of Micromegas TPCs, SiPM and plastic trigger scintillators, DREAM readout — took data at CERN n_TOF in 2026. These pages ask which other facilities could host the same hardware, possibly with a new target and modest upgrades, and how far each would reach. Everything starts analytic and is then checked against Geant4 of the full apparatus on lxplus. Work in progress, unlisted.

Where it stands (8 October 2026). LNL — the ATOMKI reaction itself, ⁷Li(p,e⁺e⁻)⁸Be, on a ~1 MeV proton beam — is the easiest and most decisive: the ATOMKI ratio at 3σ in ~2 weeks at 1 µA with the n_TOF hardware (pre-Geant4, ±×2). ILL — the thermal ³He search on a cold reactor beam — is done: feasible, but one unpolarised cycle cannot reach ⁸Be's level. GANIL/NFS stalled on the sub-2 MeV neutron flux nobody has measured.

LNL, Legnaro active

GANIL / NFS, Caen paused
