MX17 at Legnaro · the X17 test on ⁸Be

1.10 MeV protons from the AN2000 Van de Graaff hit a 300 µg/cm² Li₂O film. About one proton in 10⁹ is captured and makes ⁸Be* at 18.15 MeV, which decays by a real photon, by internal pair creation (IPC), or, if ATOMKI is right, by an X17 that gives an e⁺e⁻ pair at ≥ 133°. The four MX17 arms measure the opening angle; the spectrum below fills at the Geant4 rate.

What is real here. The histogram is sampled (Poisson, bin by bin) from the Geant4 opening-angle spectra of the overnight LNL campaign: Li₂O 300 µg/cm² on 10 µm Al, E_p = 1.10 MeV, 1 µA, selection "MM 15° + TOF", E_sum window 13–17 MeV (as built) or 13–18 MeV (big plastics). Three views of the same run: the opening angle, the opening angle for pairs that share the energy evenly (|y| = |E₁ − E₂|/(E₁ + E₂) < 0.3), and the pair mass m_ee = W √((1 − y²)(1 − cos θ)/2) with W = 18.15 MeV. The "3σ expected" tile is S/√B counting in the best window (statistics only); its small print is the fit with a 2 % unknown wiggle in the IPC shape and the in-run control samples (lnl/FEASIBILITY_SIM.md §12–13). The IPC includes its anisotropy about the beam (a₂ = 0.36). The beam-line counters use the same yields (1.85×10³ γ₀/s, γ₁ ≈ 2.06 γ₀, α_IPC ≈ 3.5–4.4×10⁻³). Cosmics after this selection are ≲ 1/day, so the histogram leaves them out; the event display shows why they would matter without the TOF and segment cuts.
What is a cartoon. The beam line, the reaction choreography and the event display. Event-display tracks are 2D, with the measured layer radii and widths (MM face 20.4 cm, SiPM wall 31.4 cm, plastics 41.2 cm, LS 55.7 cm) and a few-degree kink in the CFRP chamber wall; pinwheel offsets and the third dimension are dropped, so its acceptance is not the Geant4 one. The X17 is shown far more often than reality (about 1 in 2×10⁶ γ₀).
