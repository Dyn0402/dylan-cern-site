Proton facilities of the world

The X17 search · which proton machine could host the MX17 apparatus · a living survey, started October 2026

The ATOMKI anomaly was seen with protons: ⁷Li(p,e⁺e⁻)⁸Be, ³H(p,e⁺e⁻)⁴He and ¹¹B(p,e⁺e⁻)¹²C. Every proton machine we have found that could reach those reactions, triaged for one question: how fast would the MX17 apparatus test each claim there? Click a site on the map or a row in the table for the machine, the numbers, the verdict and how far we have looked into it.

Where it stands (22 October 2026). The target, not the beam, sets the reach. Days to 3σ fall as 1/current until the target stops taking beam: ~4 µA for a thin film (LNL CN is there already), ~20 µA with a cooled thick backing (an estimate). Above that every machine tests ⁸Be in ~1.3 h with big plastics, ~1 day as built, so no machine beats LNL by more than ×5. What separates them is which claims they reach (⁴He needs a tritium target, ¹²C needs ~1.9 MeV), cosmics and access. On top: LNGS Bellotti IBF (LUNA-MV), 1 mA and no cosmics, and JRC Geel MONNET, 100 µA with Ti-T targets routine. Six labs run their own X17 experiment. About 180 of the 267 electrostatic machines in the IAEA list could test ⁸Be at LNL-like currents.

How the numbers are made

X17 per day*

The quickest number a machine can be put on, not a reach:

X17/day = DC current [µA] × X17 per day per µA of the best claim it reaches

  • ⁸Be (1.03–1.10 MeV, Li₂O 300 µg/cm², R = 5.8×10⁻⁶): 920 per day per µA.
  • ⁴He (0.9 MeV, 2.1 mg/cm² Ti-T, B_x = 5.1×10⁻⁶): 2 000. Needs a tritium target and licence.
  • ¹²C (~1.9 MeV, 2 mg/cm² ¹¹B, B_x = 3.4×10⁻⁶): 5 000, an estimate from the 17.23 MeV resonance width.
  • Every X17 counted into 4π: no acceptance, trigger, target limit or dead time. "est." marks a current that is a class default, not a published number.

Days to ATOMKI (the figure of merit) and the 2-week reach

Beam days to 3σ at the ATOMKI strength, statistics only, from the LNL ⁸Be Geant4 (1.0 d big plastics, 17 d as built, per µA) and the Demokritos ⁴He Geant4 (3.6 d / 35 d per µA); ¹²C is scaled from ⁸Be (0.63×, estimate). Days fall as 1/current until the target saturates: 4 µA on a thin backing, 20 µA (10 µA Ti-T) on a cooled thick backing moved downstream (estimate). DREAM's live time is included and only matters near 1 mA. The 2-week reach is √(days/14) in units of the ATOMKI strength (the strength testable at 3σ in a 14-day run).
