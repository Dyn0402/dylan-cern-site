Proton facilities

The X17 facility search · proton and light-ion beams · dashboard in development, started October 2026

The ATOMKI anomaly was seen in proton-induced reactions: ⁷Li(p,e⁺e⁻)⁸Be at the 17.64 and 18.15 MeV resonances, and p + ³H → ⁴He. A proton machine makes the pairs in a thin target, with no neutron background and a much higher rate per beam hour than any neutron route. This page will become the proton counterpart of the neutron-facility dashboard.

Where it stands (21 October 2026). Two proton facilities have a study. LNL reaches the ATOMKI ratio at 3σ in ~8 days at 1 µA with the n_TOF hardware (Geant4). Demokritos has a Geant4 feasibility on the p + ³H route, limited by systematics at S/B ≈ 1 %. No world survey exists yet.
