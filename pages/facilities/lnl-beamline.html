LNL: the photographed beam line inside the MX17 arms

Pierfrancesco's photo of the line (26 Oct), modelled piece by piece and placed inside the four arms to scale. The target sits at the detector centre. The traced pairs are 160 of the 65 k accepted X17 pairs from Geant4 (run L9), followed straight through every part, with the radiation length crossed added up for each part. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Distances are read off the photo against the 38 mm steel tube, ±20 %.

As photographed

Where the target sits

0 cm

Along the beam from the detector centre (+ = downstream). The plan, and every Geant4 run, has the target at 0. Positive moves the cup and the whole line into the detector.

    Where the leptons scatter

    Pairs: share of accepted X17 pairs with a leg through the part. x/X₀ and θ₀: median over the legs that cross it, with θ₀ the Highland angle at that leg's energy. Cost: how much σ68 of the opening angle drops if the part is removed. Parts that no leg reaches are listed underneath.

    The photo, annotated

    Distances in cm upstream of the Li. The copper cup is at the left end: the beam comes from the right. The 26 Oct audit read the line the other way round. Its "end flange" is the DN63 flange to the accelerator, and the nearest steel is ~17 cm from the target, not next to it.

    Centred or offset?

    • Centred. Every Geant4 run (L2–L9) and the design put the Li at the detector centre. The ⁸Be pairs open at ~140° and leave nearly at rest (β = 0.006), so the centre gives the most pairs into two arms.
    • Moving it downstream costs pairs and buys nothing for ⁸Be. At +12 cm Geant4 keeps ×0.54–0.74 of the pairs (two configurations), at +18 cm ×0.69. Legs below 90° still cross a backing edge-on (TARGET_OPTIONS §7).
    • Moving it upstream is not simulated. By symmetry it costs about the same, and it pulls the valve and the tees into the arms.
    • So the cup sits at the centre, and the line enters from upstream between the side arms. Only the last ~3 cm of the line is in the leptons' view: a part at radius r and distance d upstream is seen only if r > 1.13 d.
    • The clash: the floor stand under the KF clamp, ~17 cm upstream, goes straight through the bottom arm's Micromegas and SiPM wall. Every support has to be ≥ 27 cm upstream (the trigger stack reaches ±25 cm), or hang from the side. The valve actuator at 37 cm clears the top arm by ~12 cm.
