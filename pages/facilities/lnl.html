LNL, Legnaro: the ATOMKI ⁸Be search with MX17

INFN LNL · AN2000 / CN · ⁷Li(p,e⁺e⁻)⁸Be · started 7 October 2026 · Geant4 8 October 2026

Repeat the original ATOMKI measurement — a ~1 MeV proton captured on ⁷Li, the 18.15 MeV state of ⁸Be decaying by internal pair creation — with the four Micromegas arms instead of plastic telescopes. Rates and yields are analytic, anchored to data; the detector is a full Geant4 simulation of the apparatus around a Li₂O target, with a selection that uses no Monte Carlo truth.

The facility

  • AN20000.2–2 MV belt Van de Graaff (1971); H⁺ ≤ 1 µA on the angled lines, 0° line ≤ 20 nA; self-service, one energy change a day
  • CN0.8–5.5 MV; 1 µA in the beam sheet at 0.8–2 MeV, ~4 µA authorised; pulsed 3 MHz, < 2 ns, ≤ 700 nA; 7 lines, one hall; Mon–Fri daytime
  • AccessLNL PAC, yearly in autumn (2025: deadline 3 Sep, meeting 8–10 Oct); contacts A. Selva, pacbeams@lnl.infn.it
  • Already done hereLNL's own ⁸Be pair spectrometer (Marchi, Góngora-Servín et al.): AN2000, 2023–24, LiF, ~800 nA, ~790 h — unpublished

The reaction

Capture makes ⁸Be at E* = 17.255 + ⅞E_p MeV. Two M1 resonances do the selecting — 441 keV → 17.64 MeV (σ 5.9 mb) and 1030 keV → 18.15 MeV — but a flat E1 direct capture runs underneath, and at 1.03–1.10 MeV it is half of the ground-state γ. E1 pairs are flat in angle, so they fill the 140° region. X17 pairs pile up above an edge at 134–139°.

The target

A few-hundred-nm film of LiF or Li₂O (or Li metal, or sputtered LiPON) on a 10–25 µm Al/C/Cu foil in a thin carbon-fibre chamber. Thin (≲ 300 µg/cm², ~60 keV), so the beam never slows into the 50× stronger 441 keV resonance — the mistake in ATOMKI's 2016 metallic-Li targets. Boron-free: ¹¹B(p,γ) puts 16–17 MeV γ in the window.

Rates and reach (Geant4, 8 Oct 2026)

At ATOMKI's conditions (Li₂O 300 µg/cm², 1.10 MeV, 1 µA): ~1.9×10³ γ₀/s and ~7 IPC pairs/s into 4π, ~900 X17/day at the ATOMKI ratio R = 5.8×10⁻⁶. Both leptons reach the Micromegas in 39 % of X17 decays, but the as-built trigger (two 20×30×2 cm plastics per arm) keeps only 3.3 %; four 75×75×5 cm slabs keep 23 %.

The energy sum separates the 15 MeV (γ₁) transitions from the 18 MeV ones — as built only with the liquid scintillators read out, cleanly with 5 cm plastics.

Cosmics are the one background that can sink it: ~8×10⁴ two-arm pairs/day in 125–155° at trigger level, against ~110 IPC. An upper E_sum edge (muons leave 20–35 MeV), then 3° Micromegas segments or a ~0.3 ns time-of-flight veto, bring them to ~1/day. The measured chambers' 15° segments are not enough. EPC, accidentals and DAQ dead time are negligible.
