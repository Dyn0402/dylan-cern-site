Details

The gain at high E_n assumes X17/IPC constant in E*: ATOMKI measured ⁴He at E* ≈ 20.5–21 MeV only. Above that the capture runs through broad ⁴He levels and nobody knows how the X17 branching evolves.

"With beam" = accidentals + the correlated beam term factorised (two-arm rate × f_vertex × f_E_sum, each event at its own θ and m_ee). The direct estimate (cuts applied event by event, 200 response draws each) gives 3.0×10⁻³ for big plastics; it rests on 1–2 MC events per bin.

TOF tagging gains ×1.5–1.8 over one untagged θ fit (5.8×10⁻³). A 2 % per-bin shape error moves the tagged reach ≤ 10 %, the untagged one ×1.9.

Details

BeamForceOperator is the forced branch of Geant4's G4BOptrForceCollision without the free-flight clone; the event weight is the total interaction probability along the line, ⟨ΣP⟩ = 0.137. StackingAction holds the primary's secondaries, predicts the arms they reach, and roulettes histories reaching 0 or 1 arm (weight 1/p) — unbiased for coincidences because whole histories are kept or dropped.

All beam two-arm events start in the beam line: Be cell window, Al end cap, Al tube window, ³He gas.

Brute force does not scale: one day of beam is 3×10¹³ neutrons. The correlated term after all cuts therefore rests on a handful of MC events (next slide).

Below 3 MeV accidentals outnumber IPC ×10–100; at 12–25 MeV the correlated beam matches it

Details

Accidentals: two single-arm clusters from different neutrons of one bunch within ±5 ns (assumed coincidence window), singles uniform in time inside each E_n bin's TOF window. Built from 138 correlated and ~32k single-arm MC clusters (big plastics). They scale linearly with the coincidence window.

Correlated beam: the factorised term weights every TOF-binned two-arm event by f_vertex = 0.26 and f_E_sum = 0.0038 (pooled, 200 response draws per event). 25 % of the two-arm events have θ in 55–110°, the X17 region.

Low bins are wide in TOF (0.2–0.75 MeV spans 1.1 µs of the 2.27 µs bunch period), so flat-in-time backgrounds (cosmics, delayed singles) pile up there.

What this does not settle yet

Physics

• X17/IPC at E* = 21–40 MeV is unknown: the whole high-E_n gain rests on it.

• Below 2.75 MeV NFS is ×20 short even before any detector upgrade pays off.

Simulation

• Correlated beam after cuts: a handful of MC events; factorised estimate used.

• Coincidence window (±5 ns) and calorimeter integration time are assumptions.

• No cosmic veto; no γ flash; no beam dump or room in the geometry.

• Ask GANIL: current at 440 kHz.
