1

Details

On 2026-09-10 the tracking QA found that one gated track in four carries a fitted charge that cannot be real: q_total above 10⁶ ADC, reaching 10³⁴, on a 12-bit ADC. Their χ² was unremarkable, so nothing had caught them. This note finds the mechanism, splits the tail into the classes it actually contains, and measures what each class does to the geometry. Nobody uses q_sum for an opening angle, but everybody uses p0, tan θ and t0.

Two samples: the campaign census (every gated track of the stage-3 full pass, no refitting) and a refit of 3,181 plane fits from run_145/stat090_0000 tag 260805_14H06_000, 300 events on each of the four arms. Everything is on the post-23-July (noisy) configuration except the pre-access runs of the census.

The fit offers charge in bins the window cannot see

The production depth grid against the readout window, for an in-time and a late track.

The fit models every waveform as Σk qk × columnk, and solves for q by NNLS with no bound.

A bin whose column is 10⁻¹² inside the window explains a 1-ADC wiggle in the last sample with q = 10¹². The model waveform barely changes, so χ² stays normal, but q_sum explodes.

The depth grid is fixed at 1080 ns after t0; the window ends at 1140 ns. Any late track has bins the data cannot see.

2

Details

The window is n_samples_per_waveform = 20 at 60 ns (run_145 run_config), so the last sample is at 1140 ns. The bundle's depth grid is n_depth_bins = 18 at 60 ns. The impulse response peaks 150 ns after a bin's charge arrives, so a bin is effectively invisible once its arrival time t0 + uk exceeds 990 ns.

A second route gives the same unobservable column: the fitted slope w carries a deep bin's centre p0 + w·uk a few mm past the edge of the strip window, where only the erf tail of its transverse spread reaches a strip. That is the space-censored class, two slides on.

Code: wft/model.py build_matrix and chi2_plane. The same edge is why q_uend rails at 1080 ns on half the gated tracks (STATUS, q_per_len withdrawn).

4

Details

The real charge (the track's pulse near 1000 ns) sits in the bins arriving at ~870–930 ns, in both fits. The production NNLS then puts 3.5×10⁹ ADC in the bins arriving after ~1400 ns. Their column peak is 10⁻¹⁰–10⁻¹⁶ ADC per unit charge, so the waveform they add is a fraction of an ADC count in the last sample. Without those bins the guarded fit reproduces the same strip.

This example is the clearest member of its class (picked by the figure script as the largest q_sum × peak-amplitude among flat time-censored runaways), so it is a picture of the mechanism, not of a typical member.

5

Details

For each runaway fit, the depth bin holding the most charge is classified. Time if its arrival plus the 150 ns impulse-response peak is after the last sample; otherwise space if its centre p0 + w·u lies outside the strip window. No runaway fell in neither class.

In the median runaway fit all but ~10⁻⁷ of q_sum sits in bins whose noise-weighted column is below 1 % of the largest. The visible charge Σ qk × column-peakk is ordinary.

The space class is a mix. Its most extreme member in the sample is a window of coherent bipolar ringing (±200 ADC, ~350 ns period, on every strip at once) that the model hardly fits. How much of the class is noise rather than steep tracks is not measured: χ² explained-fraction does not separate the classes.

6

Details

Each window is fitted twice from scratch: production, and the guarded fit, which is identical except that every NNLS solve inside the minimisation drops columns below 0.01 of the largest. "t0 moved" is |Δt0| > 5 ns. Values below 10⁻⁴ are piled at the left edge, values above 10 at the right.

The late normal tracks move too (47% beyond 0.05). Their deep bins are just as invisible; NNLS simply did not fill them past 10⁶. The q_sum threshold is a symptom marker; the class is t0.

The guard is not the right answer: on the time-censored class it is worse on χ² (median +0.035/dof), because the truncated tail carries some real signal. What it establishes is that the production geometry of late tracks depends on bins the data cannot constrain.

What this does not rule out

The guard is a probe

Worse on χ² for late tracks (+0.04/dof). It shows the production geometry depends on invisible bins, not which geometry is right.

No external reference

Neither fit was checked against scintillator pointing (det_a_scint).

One tag of one run

run_145 tag 260805_14H06_000. The census t0 dependence is campaign-wide; the per-class shifts are not.

What the late tracks are

Less often coincident than in-time tracks: probably out-of-time particles. Not established.

Noise in the space class

Coherent-ringing windows are in it, in an unmeasured fraction.

What to do

Now, no re-pass

q_sum and q_total unusable. Flag t0 > 300 ns tracks before any angle or pointing study; a late cut costs ~a fifth of the tracks.

October re-pass (O10)

Stop offering invisible bins: truncate the depth grid per fit, or a depth-continuity prior on the NNLS. Store q_obs.

Validate first

Any fix against scintillator pointing on arm A before it rides the re-pass.
