Details

The fit has a free charge per depth bin and assumes that charge is spread uniformly along the bin's piece of track. A shallow track's piece is shorter than the footprint of one electron on the strips (σ_eff ≈ 0.55 mm on det3: 0.24 mm prompt footprint, 0.4 mm diffusion at mid-gap, the strip width), so whether the charge is lumpy inside it cannot be seen. A steep track's piece is longer: the strips see 1–3 separate lumps where the model expects a smooth bar. The model cannot move charge within a bin, so the fit bends the slope and the per-bin profile to make up for it: a random angle error plus a bias toward shallow (shorter bars look more like lumps). Finer bins remove it because each bin then holds one lump or less.

The jump starts once a bin's segment exceeds ~1.5 footprint widths

Details

Onset = the angle where the excess first exceeds 0.3° (onset.py). Mean over the 30/60/90 ns runs with full ionisation: L = 1.55 σ_eff; without clustering 1.80 σ_eff (later). σ_eff² = σ0² + Dp²·u_mid + pitch²/12 ≈ 0.52–0.68 mm. On the MX17 chambers σ_eff ≈ 0.7 pitch, so "one strip" (the first answer) is numerically right, but the pitch variants show the scale is the footprint width: pitch 0.5 mm moves the onset in L/pitch from 1.14 to 1.66 but not in L/σ_eff (1.58 → 1.56); a wider footprint (0.45 mm) delays it. Without cluster-size fluctuations the excess does NOT vanish: it halves (det3 60 ns at 45°: 0.68° against 1.61°), because single electrons with Polya gain are lumpy too. So the first answer is confirmed for the mechanism, corrected on two points: the scale is the footprint, and clustering doubles the effect rather than creating it.

Details
  1. L0 floor: Ideal events (Fischle clusters, delta rays, Magboltz diffusion, Polya gain, X template, noise, ADC ceiling) fitted with the toy's own exact model, started at the truth: the floor (ws_floor).
  2. L1 production fit: The same ideal events fitted by the production reconstruction itself: wft.reco.fit_plane with the chamber's promoted bundle (physical kernel, pivot start, per-event sigma0 choice, t0 prior, dead mask).
  3. L2 footprint: Prompt X footprint 0.25 mm (SPS head-on 0.24-0.29, cloud_basics 38/42) + pseudo-Voigt tails (eta 0.10-0.36, gamma 0.53-0.91 mm, cloud_basics 23 footprint_test.json).
  4. L3 X copy: One-sided delayed copy onto the lower neighbour: det3 0.13 x the strip pulse, 114 ns late (cloud_basics 42); det2/det4 0.063, det7 0.15, det6 0 (scaled by head-on aQ).
  5. L4 gain map: Strip-to-strip gain spread, not known to the fit (bundle gain map = unit): sigma ln g det2/3 0.06, det6/7 0.09, det4 0.31 (cloud_basics 35).
  6. L5 pulse shape: Pulse narrower than the plane template: falling edge compressed so an isolated pulse leaves +6 % at the peak (cloud_basics 37). An upper estimate: the depth profile absorbs most of it on a real column.
  7. L6 extra diffusion: det3 only: extra transverse spread +235 um/sqrt(cm) over Magboltz in quadrature (cloud_basics 40). 0 elsewhere.
  8. L7 dead + CM: Dead strips the mask misses (cloud_basics 39, ws_dead_charge) + raw common mode 135 ADC per chip, removed by the production plain 64-channel median, per-channel slope spread 3.3 % (rerun/cm_study).
  9. L8 v drift: v changes over the run, fit uses the run mean: rms det2/3 0.6 %, det4 2.9 %, det6 3.5 %, det7 1.2 % (FOLLOWUPS 1, uniform drift over the run).

Details

If the excess were waveform model error it would grow with χ² and with charge (bright samples dominate both). It does neither except in the top χ² quintile. Waveform-level candidates we could not measure, put on det3 at generous sizes, alone on top of the production fit (quadrature added): H1 0.17°, H2 0.08°, H3 0.00°, H4 0.00° (H1 10 ns per-channel time skew, H2 ±5 % per-channel pulse width, H3 per-event footprint 0.20–0.45 mm, H4 the FC footprint 0.30 mm with tails). None comes near the 0.7° needed, and none raises the toy χ²/dof above ~15. The 2D tilt map, in contrast, removes 0.6° (det3) to 1.1° (det6) in quadrature, more than the 1D map along the measured coordinate (ws_widthtilt's angle_map), and det6/det7 (read in the same triggers) share part of their residual (correlation 0.15 X, 0.31 Y), which falls in the chamber centre: the reference or the mounting, as well as each chamber's own drift field.

Details

The FC decision (one σ0 = 0.30 mm for both views) is resolution-neutral in the toy (H4 on det3/det7). The per-event σ0 choice costs det4/det7 ≈ 0.1° on ideal events (L1 vs L0). The tilt map is geometry (mounting/sag/field), so like w0 it must never be transferred from the bench to n_TOF without an in-situ check.

What this does not rule out

• A waveform effect we did not think of, large enough to make 0.7° and χ² 5–10× the toy while staying blind to χ² event by event.

• The tilt at scales finer than 40 mm (cells too coarse) — the "unexplained" bar may be partly tilt.

• A larger M3 / multiple-scattering term than the 0.15° bound for low-momentum cosmics.

• Y view: the toy is X only (the Y RC spread along the strips is not simulated).

• n_TOF gas (half the diffusion) and the n_TOF angle mix (30–45°): the floor there is lower and the 60 ns jump is the main term.
