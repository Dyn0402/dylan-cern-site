Details

The target is the lab's own kind: Ti tritide, 2.1 mg/cm² of Ti loaded with tritium (T/Ti ≈ 1.5 assumed), on a backing that conducts the heat away. The beam stops in the tritide or the backing.

Coordinates as at n_TOF and LNL: beam along +Y, arms around it. The leptons that reach the arms leave the target near 90° to the beam, so the downstream ones cross the backing at grazing incidence.

Details

Neutron beams: 10⁵–10⁶ n/cm²/s at the sample (no time of flight), ⁷Li(p,n), ³H(p,n), D(d,n), T(d,n). On a 5 cm² beam and the n_TOF capsule (1.35×10²² ³He/cm²) with Viviani's σ_pair = 0.062 µb at 0.35 MeV: 359 IPC pairs/day, X17/IPC = 1.5×10⁻³.

Details

S(E) = 1.30×10⁻⁶ MeV b (1 + 25E + 38.8E²), E_cm in MeV, the fit to Hahn et al. PRC 51, 1624 (1995) in arXiv:physics/9910011. Valid 0.1–0.75 MeV; extrapolated above.

E1 IPC: the Born multipole of nTof_x17 ipc_born. Viviani et al.'s ab initio n + ³He σ_pair/σ_γ is ~2.1×10⁻³ at 0.2–1 MeV, so the true p + t IPC may be ~2× lower.

E0: single-level Breit–Wigner for the 0⁺ (Γ = 0.50 MeV, Γ_p = Γ), Γ_ee = 4.7×10⁻⁶ eV from M(E0) = 1.53 fm². An order-of-magnitude stand-in: p + t feeds the 0⁺ with ~2 b, against 5333 b for thermal n + ³He.

Details

The toy is lnl_rates' four-arm model (faces at 204 mm, 399.4 × 359.9 mm active) with the backing as a shadow or a Highland scatterer. X17 pairs are isotropic; the γ* direction is isotropic (Geant4 adds the sin²θ of E1 capture).

S/B in the best counting window (105-160°) is 1.15%. At LNL the ⁸Be edge is at ~137°, where the M1 IPC is lower, so S/B ≈ 7 %.

Details

The toy (LNL-calibrated) said 39 d / 2.3 d for Cu 50 µm and 81 d / 4.7 d for Cu 1 mm at +12 cm; Geant4 confirms the first within ×1.2–1.5 and finds the second ×2.3 better: with the target downstream 97 % of the selected pairs have both legs upstream, and the as-built trigger stack covers the upstream faces better. A θ-only fit is ×3 slower: the |y| asymmetry of the E1 IPC is what separates it from X17. EPC is 1–15 % of the background in the window (D3 has only 30–190 selected MC events; one event ≈ 40 pairs/day). ESTIMATES.md §6. Pair-tag: fraction of generated X17 pairs with two arms that each have a trigger leg (SiPM AND plastic) and an MM segment in the active area. X17/day sel.: after MM 15° + TOF and the E_sum window, at B_x(ATOMKI). The E_sum window is chosen per row to minimise the fit days (a mild look-elsewhere bias).

Details

ATOMKI's Table I: IPCC = 2.5(3), 1.0(7), 1.1(11) ×10⁻⁴ at 510, 610, 900 keV, B_x = 6.2, 4.1, 6.5 ×10⁻⁶. The Born E1 IPC coefficient at 20.5 MeV is 4.6×10⁻³ (Rose-type values are 3–5×10⁻³). Either their IPCC is a gated quantity and not IPC per γ, or the IPC in their gate is suppressed far beyond what any multipolarity allows.

Their X17 count is consistent with B_x per γ: ~10⁴ X17 produced in 100 h at 1 µA, ~300 seen at a few % acceptance. So the open question is the IPC, not the X17.

The upside row scales every background by IPCC/α_E1 at 900 keV (×1/42). It is an upper bound on how fast this could go, not a prediction.

What this does not settle yet

Open physics

• The IPC shape at S/B ≈ 1 %: redo the 3D fit and pseudo-data test for ⁴He.

• The IPC normalisation: ATOMKI’s fit vs QED (×20–45). Ask Viviani et al. for p + t IPC/γ.

• The γ* anisotropy of E1 capture is a stand-in.

• Current at 0.9–1.0 MeV after the upgrade; 0.5–0.6 MeV possible?
