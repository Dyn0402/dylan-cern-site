MX17 re-run plan

The from-scratch re-run of the cosmic-bench and SPS analysis (plan, 2026-10-23)

Goal (Dylan, 2026-10-23): one complete, consistent pass of every June cosmic-bench and SPS det4 analysis, on lxplus condor, with the physical detector model, producing every number and figure of the two detector papers. n_TOF is out of scope for these papers. The pass should run once, so everything that would force it to run again is settled first. This file is the plan; CHECKS_2026-10-23.md is the record of the checks behind it.

1 · What forces a re-run, and what does not

Only upstream choices force the full pass to be repeated: the inputs (decode, hits for seeding, M3/uRWELL reference), every calibration constant in a bundle, the reconstruction settings (waveform preparation, depth grid, solver, seeding) and the alignment. Everything downstream of the reconstructed tables — stacks, gas fits, D_T, efficiency definitions, timing, maps, figures — can be iterated after the pass for the cost of a script. The pre-run work below is therefore about the upstream list; the downstream questions (§6) are worked during and after the pass.

C8 · The calibration's reference position biases v — CONFIRMED on det3 (fleet running)

First C7 results (K = 18, σ0_x pinned 0.25): v = 41.2 (det2), 42.1 (det3), 25.3 (det6), 37.7 (det7) µm/ns. det3's 42.1 is above dry Magboltz at its field (38.5) and 20 % above what free fits read under the same kernel (§12: slope 0.96 at 36.6 → 35 µm/ns); det6 stays 33 % below det7 on the same night and field.

Cause: the ref-pinned objective fixes p0 at ref_mesh, the M3 track at the plane the hits-chain alignment (alignment_tpc_veto50, wft.calibrate line ~63) placed the detector. Hit times are compressed 20–30 % (RECONSTRUCTION_BASIS.md), so that plane is biased, and a depth offset is absorbed as dv ≈ dz / ⟨drift time⟩.

Test (rerun/precheck_vscan/v_scan.py, det3, 60 calibration events, RC constants of the rcv25 fit fixed, recal.py's cold objective):

p0 χ² at the minimum v at the minimum
pinned at ref_mesh (calibration as run) 4.60e7 39.6
profiled ±1 mm (recal.py --p0-profile) 3.07e7 (−34 %) 37.0

→ W1 changes: the reference geometry used for calibration must not come from the hits alignment. Calibrate with p0 profiled, run the reco, align on the waveform positions (mx_june_wft/01_alignment.py), and if a pinned calibration is wanted, rebuild ref_mesh from that alignment and iterate until v is stable. Every bundle made so far, r06 included, was calibrated against the hits-based reference. Fleet: condor 4538813 (det2, det3 K24, det6 and det7 at K 18 and 24).

C9 · The reconstruction's common mode moves with the signal — fix written (opt-in), validation pending

wft.io subtracts the plain per-sample median of each 64-channel block. On det7 (6-26, 495 V, FEU 08, 4000 events) the track clusters are ~17 strips wide, and against a median that masks the cluster ±3 strips:

strips of the block masked baseline error at the pulse peak, median / p90 largest error in the window, median
1–8 0 / 3.5 ADC 3.5 ADC
8–20 2.5 / 15 ADC 9–10 ADC
20–40 4 / 51 ADC 104 ADC

Under 1 % of a ~4000 ADC peak, but applied to every strip of the block: 10–25 % of a neighbour's tail, which is what the RC kernel's σ0 and D_rc, and the D_T stacks, are measured from. cloud_basics' stacks used a masked common mode; the reconstruction never did. Fix: wft.io.masked_block_median, opt-in WFT_CM_MASK=<half-width> (default 0 = bit-identical), reference-free two-pass median; on a toy (30-strip track in one block) the baseline error on the quiet strips goes from −16 to −1.3 ADC. → W11: rebuild the golden calibration caches with it and gate it (resolution, χ², σ0, D_rc) before the freeze.
