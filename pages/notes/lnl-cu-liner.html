On the flank the beam meets the wall at ~65°. A film of fixed normal thickness is ×2.4 longer along the beam, the protons leave it below 441 keV and 29–71 % of the γ₀ moves to 17.64 MeV.

Evaporated from the axis, the Li lands ∝ cos(incidence), as the Cu: ~20 µm along the beam everywhere.

Mask it to r ≤ 4 mm: beyond that the Li sits 5–14 mm upstream of the tip and the opening angle, taken from the nominal vertex, degrades (Geant4: ~25 % in days).

How to make it

all of it with tools LNL already has

Al 6061-T6 ogive

0.35–0.4 mm, collar at the base; LNL workshop, ~1 k€

→

Cu from the axis

Ar-ion etch, then 12–16 µm at the tip; a sleeve stops it at r ≈ 10 mm

→

Li from the axis

20 µm through a mask r ≤ 4 mm; same vacuum, or under Ar

→

coupons + leak test

thicknesses, tape test, 20–150 °C cycles; He leak test

→

PIGE on the beam

1.10 MeV: no Al lines (843, 1014 keV, 10.76 MeV); 478 keV present

Plating instead (any shop): double zincate, Cu strike, acid Cu 15–20 µm with an anode wire on the axis; uniform along the normal (×2.4 more Cu on the flank than needed), bake 150 °C. The Li still goes on from the axis.

Margins: 1 bar ×4.8 (0.3 mm) / ×10 (0.4 mm); tip +54–69 K at 4 µA; Cu stress ≤ 150 MPa (sputtered, room T); H in the Cu ~5 µA·day before blistering at σ 2 mm (a 3σ run needs ≲ 1).

For Pierfrancesco

Proposal

Keep your ogive; make it in Al 6061-T6, 0.35–0.4 mm.

Put ≥ 12 µm Cu (along the beam) on the inside over r ≤ 8–10 mm, from the axis.

Evaporate 20 µm Li from the axis through a mask r ≤ 4 mm.

×3.5 faster than the Cu ogive at equal current, σ68 10.5° not 18.5°.

Questions

Beam spot σ and halo at the target? (sets the Cu and mask radii)

Can the thin-film lab etch, then sputter/evaporate Cu and Li inside the ogive from the axis?

What current do you expect? (≤ 1 µA: the x-rays never matter)

Would Ti (15 µm) instead of Cu be easy? No x-rays at all, but it takes up hydrogen.
