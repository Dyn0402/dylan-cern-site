A 30–100 µm cell bias adds in quadrature to 450–560 µm, so it shows only as a position-dependent offset. That offset matters for anything comparing positions across the board or between chambers (vertexing, inter-chamber alignment), not for resolution. Angle: the quoted 1.15–2.51° already includes the asymmetry, since it is in the data. The angle scale slopes do not follow it either: det3, the strongest board, has the slope closest to 1 (0.963; §38).

det4/det6/det7 also carry a field but their maps are too sparse to correct (det4 got worse, 162 → 174). Chamber C (det6) therefore has no correction.

Do not absorb it: freeze the symmetric model and carry the correction as a systematic

Three ways to handle an effect we cannot explain

✗ Model it in the kernel

a one-sided ±1 term per board
no mechanism: best sim ×1.8, data need ×2.5–5

·

✗ Empirical correction as nominal

wft.xasym on every chamber
maps for det2, det3 only; chamber C has none

·

✓ Symmetric model + systematic

freeze r06; xasym as a ± variation; write it up
resolution unchanged; positions ±0.1 mm

The cost is fit quality (X χ², the σ0_x tension) and understanding, not the numbers we quote.

Details

Why not a kernel term. The best simulated mechanism consistent with the Y view (700 µm strips + funnelling + across-strip anisotropy at 5 MΩ/sq) gives ×1.8 of the sim baseline; the data need ×2.5–5. ρ_s below 5 MΩ/sq breaks the Y view. A fitted term would be calibrated per board and per run condition with no physics behind it.

Why not the correction as nominal. It exists for det2 and det3 June-15 only, needs the run's amplitude scale (det3 sessions 0.44–1.57) and a re-alignment, and buys no resolution.

Where it shows. The symmetric footprint cannot follow the ±1/±2 residual asymmetry (§38): the X χ² structure, and the per-event σ0_x 0.20–0.25 vs stacked 0.35–0.43.

What each workstream does instead

No new data; one simulation check bounds the angle cost

• Chamber A: session scale from n_TOF raw ADC; xasym as ± in capsule pointing / alignment / vertexing

• Chamber C: flat ±0.1 mm X systematic

Papers

• Paper 1: a subsection on the X-only, pitch-locked, board-fixed field; exclusions; leading hypothesis; position effect; mask never obtained

• Paper 2: model is symmetric in X; this is the main known mismatch; quote its angle cost

The one check (simulation, ~1 day): inject the measured one-sided ±1 term (aQ ≈ 0.165, ~110 ns late) into the paper-2 physics-floor toy, reconstruct with the symmetric model → the angle bias and resolution it costs. Expected: a few hundredths of a degree (§38 head-on +0.04°), unmeasured until it runs.

What this does not rule out

• An angle cost larger than §38 suggests, until the toy injection runs.

• A position bias on chamber C (det6) that we cannot map: carried as a flat ±0.1 mm.

• That det3’s D_T excess is the same effect — a plausible link, not tested.

• A mechanism that would also change the Y view or the gap; nothing seen there so far.
