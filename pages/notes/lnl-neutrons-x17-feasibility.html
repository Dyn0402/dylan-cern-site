Thin-stop Li (protons stop at threshold) avoids the ⁸Be resonances, but direct capture still gives ~5×10⁻⁹ γ/p (σ ~ 10 µb, ESTIMATE).

Same verdict as Demokritos: at CN, the closer you get, the more the target's IPC pairs (30,000,000/day) take over. At ≥ 20 cm they leave the acceptance, and cosmics remain.
