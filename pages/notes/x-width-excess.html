Details

The fit has no RC term in X (the resistive strips run along y), so all width growth in X with time is drift diffusion plus anything in the readout that looks like it. Details

Selection is per event on the X window maximum, which does not depend on depth, so it cannot select a drift velocity. 4

Details

Before 16 Oct the inversion sorted v and interpolated water against it. At 347 V/cm v rises from 38.8 (dry) to 41.1 at 0.4 % and falls to 35.1 at 1 %, so a measured v had two solutions, or only the wet one below dry. The sort returned a low-water value with a dry-like D_T, about 450, and so hid the excess at 347 V/cm. The 208 and 243 V/cm fits were unaffected (v is monotonic there). With det3’s 27.9 mm gap applied, v sits below dry and only the wet branch, 0.94–0.97 %, remains. That is the same as the drift-scan composition fit.

Details

Tune: per dataset, the per-event gain spread and scale are fitted so the toy’s window-maximum quantiles and saturated fraction match the data. det3: max quantiles 1286/2001/3165/3785 against the data’s 1289/2047/3024/3778, saturated 0.43 against 0.42. det7 needed a 30 % faint population, and det7R’s faintest decile is still 2× too bright. Truth: v and σ0 from the data’s unsat fit, D_T = Magboltz at the v-implied water in the GAP-30 frame.

Limits: the electronics template is the one the fit uses (template mismatch not tested), noise is Gaussian with measured correlations, and saturation is a hard clip.

Details

9

Details

A non-flat cathode tilts the drift field. In the X view the charge then walks sideways in proportion to depth, an extra angle δ the M3 reference does not see. The stack would be narrowest at tan = −δ. det4: δ +0.003 ± 0.009 with floor 337 against the toy’s 341. det7 is too sparse per bin (±60–130) to fit. The common δ ≈ +0.02 on det3 (1.3–1.5σ) could equally be a residual alignment angle.

11

Details

With a linear response r depends only on the cluster footprint, so it is flat in the peak amplitude. The toy shows what flat means with Landau and Polya fluctuations included. Only the chambers whose r departs from the toy (det4, det7) have a cut-dependent D_T. det3 follows the toy below 3000 ADC (0.34 against 0.41 at 3000–3500, just under the cut). Above the ceiling every chamber reads r ≈ 0.50–0.52 against the toy’s 0.42–0.48: real saturation widens more than clipping. Which part of the response is non-linear (the prompt footprint or the neighbour copies) cannot be decided from head-on X data.

Details

Ordered by how much each could change the kernel. The gap table (question 3) is a straight inconsistency between scripts and should be fixed first; it is cheap.

What this does not rule out

det3’s excess has no cause yet. It is not saturation, the estimator, a field tilt, the dished cathode or amplitude. Crosstalk of the bundle’s size would explain its magnitude but not why det3 alone.

Magboltz at 347 V/cm is where v(water) turns over and D_T is most sensitive to the composition. A Magboltz or cross-section error there would look like this excess.

The toy shares the fit’s template, so a template mismatch is not tested, and its saturation is a hard clip.

Decisions open

1 · Freeze the kernel now with the stated systematics, or first fix the det4/det6 gaps and requote.

2 · Whether to carry the bench Dd to the beam before run_71 is requoted on unsaturated events.

3 · Whether the det4/det7 amplitude dependence belongs in the detector model (a footprint against charge) or stays a systematic.
