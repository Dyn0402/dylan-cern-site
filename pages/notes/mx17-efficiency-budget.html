MX17 efficiency: where every track goes

June bench golden rows, physical RC model (finalize_phys tables, lxplus), pass inputs (rr1_cm0 hits, M3 v2, incomplete events out). rerun/effbudget/, 2026-10-26.

det3, interior, X+Y within 5 mm: 91.2 % detected; 93.3 % found counting the 2.1 % the competing-cluster cut removes; 95.1 % of the crossings that are not in a spark. The rest: 2.0 % sparks, 4.5 % fitted but more than 5 mm off (1.9 second particle, 0.9 δ-ray, 1.1 fit, 0.3 edge, 0.2 position tail), 0.3 % fit failed, and 0.02 % never seen or never seeded. A chamber that misses a muon outright essentially does not happen on det3.

What was counted

  • Denominator: every event with exactly one M3 track passing the recipe (χ² < 1, NClus = 4) whose 30 mm footprint lies inside the measured active area (common/mx17_active_area), inside the event range the fit table covers (det6/det7 finalize tables are the first 8000 matched events); decoder-incomplete events removed (none on these rows). Tracks leaving the area are acceptance, reported apart.
  • Detection: the waveform fit (finalize_phys, no cluster-quality cut) in both views, |r| in the raw strip frame (equal to the aligned |r| of 02_efficiency, which this reproduces: det3 far 4.45 % in both). Hits are used only for whether strips fired and where (QA), never for the point.
  • Ladder (first match): detected → removed by the competing-cluster cut (n_dropped > 2) → spark (> 50 strips above the significance floor) → no strip → known hardware → one view silent → charge not within 5 mm of the footprint → not seeded → fit failed → far. Far tracks carry their C22 class.

Reading it chamber by chamber

  • det3 and det2: no lost muons to speak of (no-hit, silent, not-seeded together < 0.1 %). The losses are physics in the chamber (second particles and δ-rays, ~3 %), sparks (2–3 %), the competing-cluster cut (2 %) and the fit (~1 %, mostly saturated tracks). At 3 mm the far share grows by ~2 pp, mostly position tail (the bent-M3 tracks of C22) and δ-rays.
  • det6: 18.5 % of its crossings sit in a spark (full-plane discharges, ~350 strips per plane). Outside sparks it finds ~92 %; its extra loss is fit failures (2.4 %, 6 % at the edge).
  • det7: sparks take 27 % of the crossings (both planes fire; the night's known spark rate) and X card 1 another 17 % (pogo shorts, the asterisk). On the live card it finds ~93 % of non-spark crossings.
  • det4: 40 % of the interior is dead or low-gain amplification band (the known stripes). In the live bands it detects 68 %, with genuine gain-limited losses: a silent view 3.7 %, charge below threshold at the track 5.6 %, fit failures 3.8 %.

What this does not rule out

  • Tables are the finalize (calibration) tables, the first 8000 matched events on det6/det7; the pass reconstructs every event and the budget should be re-run on it (it is downstream, a script).
  • "Spark" is an event-level multiplicity tag: a muon crossing during a discharge is not separable from it. Whether a spark is muon-induced (det3: 4× the random rate, June analysis) is not decided here, so "of non-spark" is an operational number, not an intrinsic one.
  • det4's band map is position-binned from the C22 per-event charge ratio (median < 0.3 in 2 mm bins of x); band edges blur over ~2 mm.
  • The far classes come from C22 (angle > 5° or position > 3 mm outliers, classified in a fixed order); a far track outside that sample is "not in the C22 sample".
  • Only the golden row of each chamber; the other nights follow in the pass.
