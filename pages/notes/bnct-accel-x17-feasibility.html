Details

The survey said the unmoderated source behind the moderator is the real prize. The physics agrees: a bare Li target is ×2–4 better in reach. The opportunity does not: in a treatment machine the target sits in the beam-shaping assembly.

The bare numbers come from the Birmingham thick-Li model (and FRANZ's for thin Li); engine reproduced to 4 figures on the published Birmingham rows (out/bnct_repro_check.csv). The moderated port replaces the catalogue's ILL-scaled 2.5e-3. Everything in bnct_accel/FEASIBILITY.md.

A moderated neutron is worth ~150× less

captures per neutron in the 30 bar cell: 10⁻⁸ below 1 keV, 1.5×10⁻⁶ at 0.5 MeV

Moderation gives ×6 more neutrons through the spot, not ×150.

Sub-keV handling: analytic captures from 0.5 eV (22 % of the epithermal captures at acceptance 0.5); background from N5's 1–3 keV bins as a proxy, ×1 and ×10.
