The recipe

  • σ0_x / σ0_y = 0.238 / 0.343 mm on every chamber: det3's per-event prompt footprint (strip box + induction ≈ 0.2 mm). The calibration fits ranged from 0.08 (det6) to 0.53 (det7).
  • Dp from the unsaturated head-on stack, √(2·Dd), which agrees with Magboltz (0.5 % H₂O): det2 0.025, det3 0.023, det4 0.020, det7 0.0225; det6 keeps 0.018 (its stack is window-limited). The bundles carried 0.028–0.032.
  • det4 D_rc,y = 2.2e-4 from the H4 beam; det6 v rescaled to the adopted 28 mm gap (24.17).
  • Per-event σ0 profile (new opt-in wft key sigma_p0_prof_<plane>): one wide alternative (0.42 / 0.50) is refitted and kept when its χ² is lower. It is taken mostly by saturated clusters, which otherwise tilt to make up their width.
