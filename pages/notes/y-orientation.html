Details

The sign of the y strip coordinate was set in software on 2026-09-07 (build_tracks.IN_PLANE_SIGN_Y = −1, applied to the POSITION). The test used then, corr(y, tan y) > 0, is satisfied equally by flipping the position or the tan, and the two choices differ by a mirror of the vertical axis. Nothing in that test fixes which one is right. This note collects five independent pieces of evidence that the position should not have been flipped.

Hover the dotted terms and points for values and counts.

How it happened: the sign test could not tell a flipped position from a flipped tan

corr(y, tan y) < 0 raw ⇒ flip one of them; 2026-09-07 flipped the position, which mirrors y

Details

Why x got it right and y did not. In x the pinwheel puts each plane centre ~16 mm off the beam axis, so flipping the tan instead of the position would have displaced opposing arms' images in opposite directions; the target image chose the position flip (2026-08-20). The y plane has no such offset, so the same choice was copied by analogy. For y it is a global mirror: both choices give a self-consistent detector, and only an external fact (wiring, gravity, a PMT position, the capsule geometry) can tell them apart.

The reco's tan convention. Since x needed a position flip but no tan flip, the reconstruction's raw tan is tan_raw = −d(strip)/d(depth) in the outward frame. Applied to a y view whose strips count upward, that means the tan must be flipped and the position left alone. The correct line is yl = +(y_p0 − 199.29) with tan_y = −tan_y_raw · k.

Details

Timing. Each wall group is read at both ends; the mean of the two end times does not depend on where along the bar the muon crossed, so it is the muon's arrival time at that wall (plus a per-arm cable constant, which shifts both medians together and cannot make a step). Beam-off DREAM triggers are on the n_TOF clock through ntof_cosmics/clock_match.py; WALA and WALC hits within ±150 ns of the matched trigger.

Selection. run_149 pairs table (results/tracking/k_run_147/pairs_*), pair AC, each track within 15° of the line joining them, |Δt| < 20 ns: 74 muons from the 35 clock-matched sub-runs. Mann–Whitney between Δy > +20 and Δy < −20: p = 4×10⁻¹¹.

Why not in-beam through-goers. The same test on late in-beam A–C straight lines gives no step: those are mostly simultaneous particles from the capsule (the calorimetry study found beam through-goers non-penetrating), which carry no direction.

Details

Method. For each track in the scint-stack table (ntof_scint_stack, every second run), the plastic bar's time minus the mean end time of the wall group with the largest two-ended signal. The wall mean removes the trigger and the particle's own arrival; what is left is the plastic's light path to its single PMT. Regressed on the track's stored v at the strip plane within each wall group (fixed effects), residuals clipped at 10 ns, bootstrap errors. The across-bar (u) slope is the control: the PMT sits at one end of the bar, not at a side.

Caveat. "PMTs on top" is Dylan's report (2026-10-06); the Geant4 model has bare bars. Amplitude trends are too weak and trigger-biased to use. The liquid scintillator timing is too noisy (n = 0.7–17 k) to add anything.

Details

Liquids. Net firing (on − off window) of the liquid behind tracks tagged by the wall at both ends. A and D lie horizontally with the PMT at +u: they answer 10–20× more on the PMT half, so the method sees light collection. B stands vertically with its PMT on top (17–18 July survey, in the Geant4 model): it answers ×2 more at stored −y. C has too few counts. This was the open "vertical liquids do not answer near their PMT" question of the scint-stack note, and the mirror answers it.

Details

The fix. build_tracks.local_and_global writes y_local = +(y_p0 − 199.29) and tany = −tan_y_raw · k. Against the 09-07 tables it is an exact global y mirror: p0_y, d_y, tany, target_y_mm, y_local change sign, and nothing else. Every stage-3 table was rebuilt from its own sidecar and checked row by row against the old one before it replaced it (regen_stage3.py). Sidecars carry y_convention: "up". A guard in every build refuses a table whose late capsule y crossing is not below zero.

What moved. Heights only: the capsule sits at the nose where the CAD and Geant4 put it (to ~5 mm; the "CAD is 30 mm off" result was the mirror), the vertical liquids rise toward their PMT, vertex-y images flip. Angles between tracks, |tan|, x and z are unchanged by construction. Full list: Y_FLIP_AUDIT.md.

What the mirror changes, and the fix

scope

  • Unchanged: every angle between two tracks (opening angles, X17 and IPC), |tan|, x and z positions, the x/z imaging, track counts and efficiencies that do not cut on y.
  • Mirrored: stored p0_y, d_y, tany sign, target_y_mm, angle_to_beam_deg, vertex heights, the capsule height and the "CAD is 30 mm off" result, CAPSULE_V / the 32.2 mm Geant4 shift, scintillator v-predictions and maps, and anything comparing heights with Geant4 or CAD.
  • Possibly affected: cuts on y windows (in-bore along y, capsule y ranges), the y-binned shear, any y-asymmetric acceptance or efficiency correction.

the fix

  1. Done 13 Oct: build_tracks keeps the position and flips tan y; a guard in every build and a test pinned to the cosmic timing.
  2. Done 14 Oct: every stage-3 set regenerated and proved an exact y mirror; consumers audited.
  3. Done 14 Oct: capsule height, same-chamber vertices, scint maps re-derived.
