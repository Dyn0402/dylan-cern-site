Details

Verdict. With the flux the lab quotes (10⁵–10⁶ n/cm²/s at the sample), the n_TOF capsule makes 504 ³He pairs per day: half of n_TOF EAR2, and 251× fewer than a 3σ test in 50 days needs with the big plastics. Pushed to the most any target could give (all the tritium, the capsule 1 cm from the beam spot), the rate rises ×27, and then the target's own p + ³H capture makes 361–722× more of the same ⁴He* pairs than the capsule. The measurement becomes the proton measurement, done worse.

How the days are made. Days to 3σ at ATOMKI's strength are scaled from the Demokritos p + t Geant4 result (same ⁴He*, same apparatus, same X17/IPC): days = 3.4 d (big plastics) or 45 d (as built) × 1.84e+06 / (³He pairs per day). This leaves out every neutron-induced background, the capsule's thick walls and the E_n spread, so it favours the neutrons. For the ILL, the same scaling gives 6 years as built, against the full ILL study's ~4,000 days at this strength (reach 1×10⁻² per 50 d): the scaling is optimistic by ~×2.

Details

The Demokritos neutron facility (Vlastou et al., IAEA; Amanatidis 2024; FACILITY.md §3) makes quasi-monoenergetic neutrons by stopping the tandem beam in a production target: ⁷Li(p,n) (120–650 keV), ³H(p,n) on Ti-T (2.0–5.3 MeV), ²H(d,n) in a gas cell (4–11.5 MeV), ³H(d,n) on Ti-T (15–21 MeV). There is no time of flight. Samples sit ~1.7 cm from the target; the quoted maximum flux there is 10⁵–10⁶ n/cm²/s at 0.7–1 µA.

The capsule is n_TOF's: the gas profile from MX17_Full_Geant DetectorConstruction.cc, 17 cm³, 1.07 g of ³He. Its nose (5.5 mm of Al) goes toward the source. The flux at every point of the gas is taken at its 0° value: a generous simplification.

Details

The physics wants E_n ≲ 2 MeV or thermal (CLAUDE.md): there the X17 edge sits at 100–111°, well above the bulk of the IPC, and the ⁴He* excitation is narrow. The deuteron sources (²H(d,n), ³H(d,n)) put E* at 23–37 MeV, where the edge is at 60–90° and the excitation is broad: out. The rest of this note uses ³H(p,n) at E_n = 2.0 MeV (E_p = 2.79 MeV at 0°) and ⁷Li(p,n) at 0.35–0.7 MeV.

Details

All per µA. The lab runs its Ti-T neutron targets at 0.7–1 µA.

Lab flux. 10⁶ n/cm²/s at 1.7 cm is the top of the quoted range, so Y₀ = 2.89e+06 n/sr/s. The capsule's nose at 1.7 cm gives G = 1.10 cm.

Best case. ³H(p,n): all 4.0×10¹⁹ T/cm² of the lab's Ti-T at σ = 0.6 b (Brune et al. 1999 give 0.5–0.6 b near E_p = 2.8 MeV), forward-peaked ×2 in the c.m. and ×2.0 by the Jacobian: Y₀ = 4.77e+07 n/sr/s, ×16 the quoted flux. ⁷Li(p,n): a thick metallic Li target at 2.5 MeV, ~9×10⁸ n/s/µA (Lee & Zhou 1999), rounded to 10⁹ and ×3 forward. Both with the capsule's gas tip 1.0 cm from the beam spot (1 mm backing, cooling, 5.5 mm Al nose).

Moderated. Thermalising the neutrons helps nothing: even if every source neutron were absorbed in ³He, 4.8×10⁻¹¹ pairs per absorption gives 615 (³H(p,n)) or 4,147 (thick Li) pairs per day. The E0 channel adds tens of percent at thermal.

Needed. The Demokritos p + t Geant4 reach, 3.44 d at 1.84e+06 pairs/day with big plastics, scaled to 50 days: 1.27e+05 pairs/day (1.67e+06 as built).

Details

Days scale as 1/I as long as nothing else changes. Ti-T neutron targets are run at 0.7–1 µA because the tritium leaves a hot target; at E_p = 2.8 MeV, 251 µA is 703 W into a ~1 cm² spot.

The best-case lines need 9 µA (³H(p,n)) or 3 µA (thick Li) for 50 days. They are not achievable in practice (the capsule's gas 1 cm from a water-cooled target, all of the target's yield counted, every factor rounded up), and even granting them, the next slide shows the target's own pairs dominating.

Details

³H(p,n) source. The same protons that make the neutrons capture on the target's tritium: ³H(p,γ)⁴He, E* = 21.90 MeV at E_p = 2.79 MeV. Two estimates of σ(p,γ): the Hahn S-factor fit extrapolated (76 µb), and detailed balance on ⁴He(γ,p)³H with σ(γ,p) = 0.6–1.2 mb near 22 MeV (Shima et al. PRC 72 (2005) 044004; Raut et al. PRL 108 (2012) 042502; the data disagree by up to ~50 %), giving 49–98 µb. With the Born E1 pair fraction 4.72e-03: 4.9e+06–9.9e+06 pairs/day, against 1.37e+04 from the capsule: ×361–722.

The two vertices are 1–7 cm apart. MX17 cannot separate them: the MM pointing cut accepts tracks within 135 mm of the assumed vertex, and the angular resolution is 9–14°. So the target's pairs are signal and background in the capsule's analysis, with the same X17 edge.

4π shell bound. Even if every source neutron crossed L cm of 500 bar ³He: 2.3e+04 / 1.2e+05 / 2.3e+05 pairs/day for L = 1 / 5 / 10 cm (5 / 88 / 452 g of ³He), still ×211 / ×42 / ×21 below the target.

⁷Li(p,n) source. A thick Li target at 2.5 MeV captures through the 441 keV (17.64 MeV) and 1030 keV (18.15 MeV) resonances on the way down: 3.69e-08 γ₀ + 6.10e-08 γ₁ per proton (the LNL cross sections; direct capture held flat above the last measured point). Those are ⁸Be pairs at E* = 17.6–18.2 MeV, ×4,402 the capsule's. Their E_sum is 3–4 MeV lower, but the arms contain ~40 % of the lepton energy, so an E_sum cut cannot remove ×10³.

verdict

Close the neutron-beam option at Demokritos. If Demokritos, then protons on its tritium.

Why it is closed

• The lab’s flux gives 504 ³He pairs/day: 34 years to 3σ with big plastics.

• Moderating to thermal gives no more: ≤ 615 pairs/day even if every neutron is absorbed.

• A source strong enough makes ×361+ more pairs in its own target, 1 cm away.

• Every neutron-induced background was left out; each would make it worse.

What this does not rule out

• p + ³H on the lab’s Ti-T at 0.9 MeV, below the (p,n) threshold: 3.4 d with big plastics, 45 d as built (Geant4, statistics only).

• ³He with neutrons where the flux is 10⁴× higher and no target sits next to the cell: the ILL, GANIL-NFS.

• Nothing here needs the lab’s input: the numbers are the lab’s own quoted flux and physics.
